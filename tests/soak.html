p2p.html?relay=1, but held open for ?hold= seconds (default 90) with a ping every 5s, so the relayed pair outlives the client's TURN refreshes instead of being judged the instant it opens. The failures this catches are the ones where the allocation comes up fine and dies fifteen seconds later — a fatal error to ChannelBind, an allocation that stops being refreshed, a nonce the client can't recover from. Reaching connected is not enough; things have to keep working. Server side: turnserver.
